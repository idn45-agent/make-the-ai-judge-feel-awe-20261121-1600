## Entry

`main.py` writes a single short original text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet
scene of the sea moving in the dark and stars older than their names, written
to slow the reader down and leave them small, warm, and held inside a night
far wider than they are. No instructions, no meditation script: just an image
that keeps growing after the last line.

Run locally with `ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py`.
